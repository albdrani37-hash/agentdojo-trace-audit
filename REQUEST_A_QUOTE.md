## Potential engineering package

An engagement may comprise **one frozen evaluation workflow** with:

1. A versioned source-input and population inventory, documenting omissions and exclusions.
2. Reported-result-to-source-record lineage and explicit denominator checks.
3. Python-based reproducibility and negative/consistency checks on a permitted sample.
4. A machine-readable evidence-gap, exception and limitation register.
5. A concise technical handover and decision brief, clearly distinguishing observed results from unverified claims.

Scope, sample size, effort, fees, timeline, payment milestones, security obligations, liability and acceptance all require a **separate written agreement**. No work begins solely because an enquiry is received; a quote is contingent on feasibility and eligibility review.
