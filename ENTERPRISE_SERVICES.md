## Who this is for

Engineering, evaluation, reliability, and AI assurance teams that operate or test agentic workflows and need to reconcile **reported evaluation outcomes with the underlying recorded executions** before making internal release or procurement decisions.

The work concerns evidence integrity and reproducibility; it is **not** a certificate of model safety or regulatory compliance.

## Proposed bounded engagement

After a confidential, non-sensitive scoping discussion and an agreed written statement of work, an engagement may cover:

- **Frozen input inventory:** identify evaluation dataset versions, units of analysis, selected population, explicit exclusions, missing records, and source hashes.
- **Claim-to-evidence lineage:** show which traces, definitions, transformations, and aggregations support the selected reported results.
- **Population and denominator reconciliation:** reproduce reported counts and flag ambiguous or contradictory inclusion rules.
- **Executable checks:** deliver documented Python verification code, test fixtures, run instructions, and a machine-readable results summary.
- **Limitations and decision brief:** distinguish confirmed discrepancies from `UNKNOWN`/`HOLD` states and state exactly what can and cannot be concluded.

A potential initial scope is **one specified agent-evaluation workflow and one frozen dataset**, with any record limits, timeline, fee, and acceptance criteria agreed only after technical sizing. Data access requires appropriate authority and handling terms; do not email restricted data during initial contact.
